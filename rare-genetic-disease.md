## Burosumab

Burosumab is a monoclonal antibody that blocks fibroblast growth factor 23, commonly known as FGF23. It is used in the treatment of X-linked hypophosphatemia and certain eligible cases of FGF23-related hypophosphatemia.

Burosumab is supplied as a subcutaneous injection in different single-dose vial strengths. The prescribed strength, number of vials, storage requirements and treatment schedule can vary according to the individual clinical assessment.
