Specialised immunology treatments may be considered when conventional approaches are unsuitable or have not provided the required response. Selection depends on the diagnosed condition, previous treatment, clinical findings and the prescribing specialist’s assessment.

## Canakinumab

Canakinumab is a monoclonal antibody that blocks interleukin-1 beta, a protein involved in inflammatory responses. It is used in selected periodic fever syndromes, active Still’s disease and certain other prescribed treatment settings.

The medicine is supplied as a subcutaneous injection. Its suitability, dose and treatment interval depend on the diagnosed condition, body weight and clinical response.

## Emapalumab

Emapalumab is an interferon gamma-neutralising antibody administered by intravenous infusion. It is used in selected patients with primary haemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis and certain HLH or macrophage activation syndrome settings associated with Still’s disease.

Treatment requires specialist supervision, infection screening, laboratory monitoring and careful preparation of each infusion. Availability may depend on the prescribed strength, number of vials and sourcing route.

## Revumenib

Revumenib is an oral menin inhibitor developed for specific forms of relapsed or refractory acute leukaemia identified through molecular testing. Patient selection is based on eligible KMT2A alterations or NPM1 mutations and the treating specialist’s assessment.

The medicine is available in multiple tablet strengths. Treatment requires careful monitoring because the prescribed dose and supporting measures can vary according to body weight, concurrent medicines and clinical response.
