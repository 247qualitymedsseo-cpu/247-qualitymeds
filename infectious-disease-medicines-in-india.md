## Pentamidine Isethionate

Pentamidine isethionate is an anti-infective medicine used in specific clinical circumstances involving Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia (PCP). This opportunistic infection primarily affects people with significantly weakened immune systems. The systemic injectable formulation may be considered for active PCP treatment when clinically appropriate, including when other established therapies are unsuitable or not tolerated.

Pentamidine isethionate for injection is supplied as **300 mg of lyophilised powder in a single-dose vial** and is administered intravenously or intramuscularly following reconstitution. Packages may contain 10 single-dose vials and should be stored at **20°C to 25°C**.

A separately formulated inhaled version of pentamidine may be used to help prevent PCP in selected high-risk patients. The inhaled and injectable formulations have different clinical purposes and should not be considered interchangeable. The formulation, dosage, route of administration, reconstitution procedure and monitoring requirements must be determined by a qualified healthcare professional.
